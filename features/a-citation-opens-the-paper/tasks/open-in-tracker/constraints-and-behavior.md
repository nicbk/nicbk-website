# Constraints and Behavior: Open in Tracker

The feature's Behavior, plus:

- **Absent, not disabled**, when the region matches nothing, when the matched
  edge has no article, or when the link is not a reference.
- **The link is a link** — a real anchor, openable in a new tab like the
  citations view's rows — carrying `via` from `~/lit-tracker/citation-path` and
  dropping `view`.
- It sits beside *go to p. N*, which keeps its place and behaviour.

Feature criteria 1, 2, 3 and 6.
