# Constraints and Behavior: The Toolbar Opens the Contents

The feature's "The button" behavior, plus:

- **One list, two ways to reach it.** The button selects the existing tab. It
  does not open a second outline surface.
- **The toolbar keeps its row count at every width.** At 375px it is two rows
  today. The button's place is measured, not assumed, and if it does not fit
  beside the page field the measurement decides where it goes.
- **It carries a word and a glyph like the toolbar's other triggers**, dropping
  the word at the same breakpoint they do.
