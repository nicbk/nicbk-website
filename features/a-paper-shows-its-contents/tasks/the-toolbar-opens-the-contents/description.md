# Task: The Toolbar Opens the Contents

Task 4 of 4 of [#29](../../description.md). Sub-issue [#248](https://github.com/nicbk/nicbk-website/issues/248).

A *contents* button beside the reader toolbar's page field. It opens the sidebar
on the contents tab: the rail on a wide screen, the sheet on a narrow one. On a
phone, choosing an entry closes the sheet.
