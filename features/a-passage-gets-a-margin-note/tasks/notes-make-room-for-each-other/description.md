# Task: Notes Make Room for Each Other

Task 2 of 2 of [#28](../../description.md). Sub-issue [#244](https://github.com/nicbk/nicbk-website/issues/244).

Turns task 1's refusals into re-arrangements where it can:
- other margin notes in that margin move, vertically and within 48pt of their own
  passages, to fit a new note;
- a note's height fits its text when editing ends, and the margin re-arranges
  around it;
- selecting a note or its passage shows both.
