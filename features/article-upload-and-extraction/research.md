# Research Traceability: Article Upload and Extraction

Every decision this feature implements traces to an already-decided
`research/*.md` doc or a `high-level-guidance/design/*` artifact. No decision is
improvised here; the narrow, feature-local choices (scope calls the research
left open, and facts that changed between the July research and implementation)
are recorded in the "Notes" section below rather than left implicit — per
[../../research/project-management-conventions/feature-definition-and-scoping.md](../../research/project-management-conventions/feature-definition-and-scoping.md).

## High-level design

- [../../high-level-guidance/design/lit-tracker/DESIGN.md](../../high-level-guidance/design/lit-tracker/DESIGN.md)
  — the origin requirement: "User simply uploads an article (e.g. PDF) and then
  the app automatically extracts relevant information (e.g. name, abstract,
  authors, bibliography) to add to the user's collection", every upload starting
  at `pending` reading status, and the site-wide native-reactivity rule applied
  to this sub-app ("live updated across all live clients").
- [../../high-level-guidance/design/DESIGN.md](../../high-level-guidance/design/DESIGN.md)
  — open-source-only components (which is why Garage and GROBID, not S3 and a
  commercial extraction API), **shared infrastructure across sub-apps** (one
  Postgres, one blob store, one sync engine), and native reactivity as a
  site-wide constraint rather than a lit-tracker feature.

## Sync engine, reactivity, and authorization

- [../../research/technologies/sync-engine.md](../../research/technologies/sync-engine.md)
  — **Zero (Rocicorp)**, Apache-2.0, read *and* write sync with optimistic
  mutations; requires Postgres with `wal_level = logical`.
- [../../research/system-architecture/reactivity-propagation.md](../../research/system-architecture/reactivity-propagation.md)
  — the full path (Postgres → zero-cache → client over WebSocket), `useQuery`
  auto-re-rendering, **authorization only at subscription time**, and the
  confirmation that sync is **write-origin-agnostic** — a pg-boss job handler
  updating `upload_jobs` propagates exactly like a user mutation. This is what
  makes the status popup live with no special plumbing.
- [../../research/system-architecture/data-sharing-boundaries.md](../../research/system-architecture/data-sharing-boundaries.md)
  — **multi-tenant**: only Better Auth's identity tables are shared; everything
  else is sub-app- then user-scoped by a `user_id` FK, **enforced in the app
  server's `/query`/`/mutate` handlers** because Zero has no RLS-style
  permissions layer. This is the single most load-bearing citation in the
  feature.
- [../../research/system-architecture/service-topology.md](../../research/system-architecture/service-topology.md)
  — zero-cache as a separate always-on process in **single-node mode**; Garage
  single-node; GROBID a long-lived container on the same host (~4 GB RAM);
  Semantic Scholar external.

## Background pipeline

- [../../research/system-architecture/background-jobs.md](../../research/system-architecture/background-jobs.md)
  — **pg-boss**; **separate chained jobs one per stage** (extract → enrich →
  finalize) rather than one job with a `stage` field; **non-fatal** Semantic
  Scholar failures; the app-owned **`upload_jobs`** table as the thing Zero
  replicates (pg-boss's own `pgboss` schema is a private implementation detail,
  excluded from the publication); **transactional enqueue** via pg-boss's
  first-party Drizzle adapter; and the explicit distinction between a transient
  failure (pg-boss retries) and a genuinely corrupt PDF (terminal, caught by the
  handler).

## Schema

- [../../research/data-modeling/zero-schema-conventions.md](../../research/data-modeling/zero-schema-conventions.md)
  — client-generated **UUIDv7** PKs, `timestamptz` exposed as epoch-ms
  `number()`, **hard deletes**, `ON DELETE CASCADE` on ownership FKs, and the
  schema declared once in Drizzle with `zero/schema.ts` generated by
  `drizzle-zero`.
- [../../research/data-modeling/article-core-schema.md](../../research/data-modeling/article-core-schema.md)
  — the `articles` table column by column: `authors` as jsonb (not normalized),
  the generated `authors_search` column with its `pg_trgm` GIN index (and the
  caveat that reactive search never touches it), `status` as a plain column
  rather than tag rows, the persisted `extraction_status` enum, the
  `lit-tracker/{user_id}/{article_id}/source.pdf` object-key convention with
  **no URL persisted**, and the nullable `semantic_scholar_id` for per-user
  matching.
- [../../research/data-modeling/upload-jobs-schema.md](../../research/data-modeling/upload-jobs-schema.md)
  — `status` as only `'processing' | 'failed'` with **no terminal completed
  value** (resolved rows are deleted outright); the article row created on
  extract completion **whether it succeeds or fails**; and the
  **pre-allocated-ID** design that lets the PDF land in Garage before the
  article exists (`upload_jobs.id` is the future article's ID, generated by
  Postgres's native `uuidv7()` — the one documented exception to
  client-generated PKs).
- [../../research/data-modeling/citation-graph-schema.md](../../research/data-modeling/citation-graph-schema.md)
  — `citation_edges` (not `references`, a reserved keyword); a placeholder is
  just a row with a null `cited_article_id`, and graduation is a plain `UPDATE`;
  **S2-`paperId`-first matching with a normalized title + first-author
  fallback**, run in **both directions** (new article scans unresolved edges;
  new edge scans existing articles); `ON DELETE SET NULL` on
  `cited_article_id`; delete-and-reinsert idempotency on re-extraction.
- [../../research/data-modeling/tags-and-reading-status.md](../../research/data-modeling/tags-and-reading-status.md)
  — why reading status is `articles.status` and not a tag row, and that
  `tags`/`article_tags` are reserved for real user-defined tags (so they belong
  to #8, not here).
- [../../research/technologies/database.md](../../research/technologies/database.md)
  — **Postgres v18+**, one shared instance; the version floor exists because
  generated `stored` columns only replicate to Zero on 18+.
- [../../research/technologies/orm.md](../../research/technologies/orm.md)
  — **Drizzle + Drizzle Kit** own DDL; `zero/schema.ts` generated via the
  official `drizzle-zero`.
- [../../research/devops-deployment/database-migrations.md](../../research/devops-deployment/database-migrations.md)
  — migrations complete before the app starts (the one-shot `migrate` service
  #6 substituted for `pre_start`), **expand/contract discipline** mandatory.

## Storage and extraction services

- [../../research/technologies/blob-storage.md](../../research/technologies/blob-storage.md)
  — **Garage**, S3-shaped, over SeaweedFS; and the standing warning that MinIO
  is archived and must not be reached for.
- [../../research/security-privacy/pdf-and-annotation-data-protection.md](../../research/security-privacy/pdf-and-annotation-data-protection.md)
  — **all PDF reads and writes proxied through the app server; no presigned
  URLs ever issued**, so file access is authorized in the same place as every
  other piece of user data. At-rest encryption is **host-level LUKS** on the
  Garage partition (see Notes — a deployment concern, not application code).
- [../../research/technologies/pdf-metadata-extraction.md](../../research/technologies/pdf-metadata-extraction.md)
  — **GROBID** (Apache-2.0, self-hosted, never the public demo server) for
  extraction; **Semantic Scholar only, no Crossref**, because arXiv registers
  DOIs with DataCite and Crossref's arXiv coverage is a known dead zone.
- [../../research/technologies/citation-graph.md](../../research/technologies/citation-graph.md)
  — the directed-graph model whose edges this feature writes (the traversal UI
  itself is #10).

## Notes / narrower research (feature-local, not global)

- **Verified current at spec time (2026-08-01).** Per
  [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)'s research-over-recall rule, the moving parts
  were re-checked rather than trusted from the July research: `@rocicorp/zero`
  **1.8.0** (Apache-2.0), `drizzle-zero` **0.20.0** — now published from
  **`rocicorp/drizzle-zero`**, confirming `orm.md`'s "official generator"
  description — `pg-boss` **12.26.4** (MIT), `@aws-sdk/client-s3` **3.1101.0**.
  All actively published.
- **zero-cache needs three Postgres connections, not one** (new since the July
  research). `ZERO_UPSTREAM_DB` (direct, not via a pooler — it owns the
  replication slot), `ZERO_CVR_DB` (client view records), and `ZERO_CHANGE_DB`
  (replication log). These are **separate databases inside the existing shared
  Postgres service**, not a second Postgres — the shared-infrastructure
  constraint is unaffected, but the compose service and `.env.example` have to
  account for all three. Also required: `ZERO_REPLICA_FILE` (a persisted volume
  for the SQLite replica) and `ZERO_ADMIN_PASSWORD`.
- **Zero's current model makes the decided design the mainstream path.** Zero
  1.x has moved to **synced queries** and **custom mutators**, with the legacy
  CRUD query/mutate paths off by default (`ZERO_ENABLE_CRUD_MUTATIONS=false`;
  `drizzle-zero` exposes `--enable-legacy-mutators`/`--enable-legacy-queries`
  opt-ins). The `/query` endpoint receives a query name plus client arguments
  and a **server-derived `context` the client cannot modify**, and returns ZQL
  — which is precisely
  [data-sharing-boundaries.md](../../research/system-architecture/data-sharing-boundaries.md)'s
  decision that user scoping is enforced in the handler. So this feature builds
  on the current, non-legacy path; **do not enable the legacy flags**.
- **Semantic Scholar needs no API key.** Unauthenticated access works against a
  rate limit shared across all unauthenticated callers; an individual key grants
  a dedicated ~1 req/s. So `SEMANTIC_SCHOLAR_API_KEY` is an **optional**
  environment variable, sent when present. At personal-collection volume the
  unauthenticated pool is expected to suffice, and enrichment failure is
  non-fatal by design, so a throttled response degrades to `grobid_only` rather
  than breaking an upload.
- **Upload limits are a feature-local choice, not a research decision.** No
  decided doc sets a size or count cap. Proposed and to be confirmed at review:
  **50 MB per file**, **20 files per submission**, `application/pdf` **plus a
  `%PDF-` magic-byte check** (the declared content type is attacker-controlled
  and not trusted on its own). These are chosen to be comfortably above a real
  paper's size while bounding what one request can push into Garage and GROBID;
  adjust freely — they are configuration, not architecture.
- **`/mutate` ships with an empty mutator registry (scope call).** Nothing in
  this feature writes from the browser: the PDF upload is a proxied REST
  endpoint because a binary is not a mutator payload, and every other write is
  made server-side by a job handler. Rather than invent a throwaway client
  mutation, the endpoint is stood up real and correctly authorized with no
  mutators registered; #8's reading-status and tag writes are its first
  consumers. Same reasoning #6 used for the route guard.
- **The failure path is deliberately incomplete.** `upload-status.md` resolves a
  failed job through `article-edit`, which is #11 and depends on #7. This
  feature ships the warning row and the article behind it; the row clears when
  #11 lands. Confirmed with the user rather than worked around — see
  [description.md](./description.md).
- **LUKS is a deployment task, not application code.** The at-rest encryption
  decision is host-level LUKS on the Garage partition. Nothing in this repo can
  enforce it, so it is recorded here as a **nicbk-tower provisioning
  prerequisite** to be done before real PDFs are stored in production. Also
  worth carrying into that deploy: GROBID's ~4 GB RAM footprint alongside
  zero-cache and Garage, and extending backup coverage
  ([backup-and-disaster-recovery.md](../../research/devops-deployment/backup-and-disaster-recovery.md))
  to the Garage volume — this is the first data on the site that cannot be
  rebuilt from the repository.
- **`pgboss` must be excluded from the Zero publication.** Not a preference:
  pg-boss's internal tables are documented as unstable across versions, and
  Zero's replication scope is controlled by the Postgres publication rather than
  by what `zero/schema.ts` declares. Getting this wrong replicates churn to
  every client and couples us to a private schema.
