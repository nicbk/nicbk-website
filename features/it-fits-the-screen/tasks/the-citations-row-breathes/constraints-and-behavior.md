# Constraints and Behavior: The Citations Row Breathes

The feature's **citations row** constraints, plus:

- **Narrow only.** The rule is declared for the tier where the sidebar is a
  sheet rather than a column; the desktop row is untouched and its rule still
  meets the sidebar's at 0px.
- **Equal above and below**, measured from the controls rather than from the
  tabs: the controls are what a reader sees crowding the line.
- The drawer button keeps its size — it is the touch target on the device that
  has it.
